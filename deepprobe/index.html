The Philosophy of v2

Correlation reduces noise better than detection

Adding more rules increases coverage, but linking findings provides the context needed for high-confidence decisions.

Multi-layer visibility improves confidence

Individual indicators are ambiguous. When activity spans process, kernel, and network layers simultaneously, compromise becomes undeniable.

AI requires strict forensic boundaries

In security workflows, hallucination is unacceptable. DeepProbe AI is constrained to act only as an interpreter of existing evidence.
