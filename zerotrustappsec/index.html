Core Value Propositions

Evidence Confidence

Measures the "I Don't Know" answers to provide a confidence interval. A 100% score based on guesses is less valuable than a 60% score based on evidence.

Zero-Config Portability

Single HTML file. No database. No API calls. Your security data stays in your browser. Perfect for offline assessments and high-security air-gapped environments.

Prioritized Roadmap

Generates a list of targeted recommendations based on your lowest maturity scores, helping you build a practical 12-month improvement strategy.
